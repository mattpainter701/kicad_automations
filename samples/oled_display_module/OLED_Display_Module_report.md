## Design Rationale

### U1 — AP2112K-3.3

- VDD_3P3: 3.3V from VBUS_5V at 0.6A (AP2112K-3.3)
- Dropout: 0.250V, Pdiss: 1.02W, Iq: 55uA
- WARNING: Pdiss=1.02W > 500mW — needs heatsink

### U4 — MCP1700-1802E

- VDD_1P8: 1.8V from VDD_3P3 at 0.25A (MCP1700-1802E)
- Dropout: 0.178V, Pdiss: 0.37W, Iq: 1.6uA

### U2 — SSD1306

- Display SSD1306: 128x64 OLED, I2C
- Reset RC delay: 10k * 100nF = 1.0ms
- Interface: I2C (DC=GND -> addr 0x3C)
- IREF: 909k (segment current set)
- Charge pump caps: 2x 2.2uF

### U3 — TXS0102

- Level shifter: TXS0102 (2ch)
- VCCA=VDD_1P8, VCCB=VDD_3P3

### RP1 — PULLUPS_ONLY

- I2C pull-ups: 3.6k to VDD_3P3 (3.3V)
- Speed: 400kHz, C_bus=100pF
- R = t_rise / (0.8473 * C_bus) = 3541 -> 3.6k
